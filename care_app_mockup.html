Can I use pills while breastfeeding?
Is the pill safe?
Yes! Progestin-only pills are safe during breastfeeding. Combined pills may reduce milk supply slightly. Discuss with your healthcare provider.

Sources: WHO 2022

Myth Buster

Real facts about contraception

❌ This is a myth

"Pills make you infertile"

Pills are reversible. Fertility returns within 1-3 months of stopping. Multiple studies confirm no long-term effects.

📚 Evidence: WHO 2022 Contraceptive Guidelines, CDC Fertility Research
